## ⚖️ Relevant Paragraffer (Legal Code)

These are the specific laws that enable the "Welfare Safe Mode".

### 1. Serviceloven § 18 (Støtte til frivilligt socialt arbejde)
*   **The Law:** Kommunalbestyrelsen skal samarbejde med og yde støtte til frivillige sociale foreninger.
*   **Application:** This is the primary funding source for "Operational Subsidy" (Mad, Lokaler, Aktiviteter).
*   **Key Phrase:** "Indsatsen skal komme socialt udsatte til gode."

### 2. Lov om socialfrikort (The cornerstone of liquidity)
*   **The Law:** Borgere med særlige sociale problemer kan tjene op til 41.280 kr. (2024) skattefrit uden modregning i kontanthjælp.
*   **Application:** Allows Mik, Sofie, and Tomas to legally extract cash for work performed.
*   **Condition:** Requires municipal visitation ("Målgruppevurdering").

### 3. Ligningsloven § 7 M (Skattefri godtgørelse)
*   **The Law:** Ulønnede bestyrelsesmedlemmer og frivillige kan modtage skattefri godtgørelse til dækning af udgifter (telefon, internet, administration).
*   **Application:** Used to cover "Soft Costs" (Internet for Tomas, Transport for Mik).
*   **Limit:** Check current rates (approx. 2.450 kr. for phone/internet annually + limitless for documented transport).

### 4. Momsloven § 13, stk. 1, nr. 4 (Momsfritagelse)
*   **The Law:** Foreninger er fritaget for moms, hvis salget af varer/ydelser sker i forbindelse med velgørende arrangementer, og overskuddet går til foreningens formål.
*   **Application:** Allows tax-free ticket sales/bar sales (up to 50.000 kr. registration limit).

## 📂 Støtte Sager (Precedents / Case Types)

Examples of project types that historically receive funding. Shape your narrative to fit these molds.

### Case A: "Det Kulturelle Værested" (The Mik Model)
*   **Concept:** A place where vulnerable citizens meet to play music or create art.
*   **Funding Argument:** Art is a non-threatening way to break isolation. It builds confidence and skills.
*   **Expenses Covered:** Instruments, Studio Rent, Guest Teachers.

### Case B: "Fællesspisning mod Ensomhed" (The Sofie Model)
*   **Concept:** Weekly dinners for people who eat alone.
*   **Funding Argument:** Nutrition and social contact are fundamental needs.
*   **Expenses Covered:** Food (Råvarer), Kitchen Equipment, Transport for cooks.

### Case C: "Brobygning til Foreningslivet"
*   **Concept:** Helping isolated individuals join existing communities.
*   **Funding Argument:** Prevents costly relapses into homelessness/hospitalization.
*   **Expenses Covered:** Mentoring, Activity expenses.

## ⚠️ Compliance Checklist

Before referencing these laws, ensure:
1.  **Vedkægter:** The association's statutes explicitly state a "Socialt og Almennyttigt Formål".
2.  **Regnskab:** Accounts are transparent and audited (even if internally initially).
3.  **Adskillelse:** No private expenses mixed with association expenses.
